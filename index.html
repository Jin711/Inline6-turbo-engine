Inline-6 — turbo x-ray

A 3.0 L square inline-six (86 × 86 mm) with a single turbo and every casing drawn as glass. Six pistons share one crank, firing every 120° in the order 1-5-3-6-2-4; the twin cams run at half crank speed. Exhaust leaves through the log into the turbine; the compressor takes cold air, heats it, and the front-mounted intercooler cools the charge before the plenum. Raise the RPM to watch the turbo spool, the boost build, and the charge flow quicken.

The block, head and plenum are glass; the crank, rods, pistons, valves, cams and chain stay solid inside. Six cylinders fire every 120° in the order 1-5-3-6-2-4. The exhaust log feeds the turbine, whose shaft drives the compressor on the cold side; the hot charge crosses the top to the intercooler, then drops into the plenum and ports. The three live cards track cylinder 1's stroke and spray and the turbo's boost; they are fixed in the drawing, with only their leader lines following the charge, spray and turbo. Raise RPM to spool the turbo and open the throttle.
